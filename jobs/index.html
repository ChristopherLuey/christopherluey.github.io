Questions.

How do I post a job?

, each with its own salary, referral bonus, eligibility rules, and payout timing. Christopher reviews each posting before it goes live. Your contact details remain private.

Can I put myself forward?

Yes. Open a role and choose “I’m interested.” A referral bonus is for an eligible referrer; applying yourself does not automatically qualify.

What happens after an introduction?

Your note goes to my private inbox. I’ll review the fit and can follow up by email. An introduction or interview isn’t guaranteed.

How do referral bonuses work?

Employers pay referrers directly. Each bonus-backed role lists the amount, who qualifies, and what must happen before payment. Read those terms before making a referral. A submission alone does not earn a bonus. Listings marked “Bonus to be confirmed” do not yet have a confirmed bonus offer.

How do I share a referral link?

Open a role and choose “Share referral link.” Add your name and email or phone, then copy your personal link. Anyone who applies through it is connected to you in Christopher’s private inbox. You can send the same link to several people.

Can I refer more than one person?

Yes. Share your link with each person, or send separate introductions with their permission.

Who posts the roles?

I handpick roles from founders and teams in my network. Employers can also submit roles and bonus terms for review.
